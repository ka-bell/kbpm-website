# KB+PM Website – Phase 1: Service Architecture

**Status:** Approved (with note below).

---

## Core note

These are **not** service packages.

They represent the **typical lifecycle of a digital product** and the **different moments clients engage KB+PM**.

---

## Objective

Create a logical, non-overlapping service architecture that becomes the foundation for the website.

Phase 1 is **not** about refining content, naming, copy, UI or navigation.

---

## Out of scope (do not do yet)

* Marketing copy
* UI / visual design
* Navigation
* Mega menu
* Homepage layout
* Naming optimisation
* Capability list polishing

---

## In scope

For each phase, define only:

1. **When does a client come to us?**
2. **What is the goal of this phase?**
3. **Which capabilities belong here?** (every capability may exist in one place only)
4. **Concrete examples of work** (not case studies — e.g. "Headless website", "AI publishing platform", "Customer portal")
5. **The outcome for the client**

---

## Architecture rule

Every capability appears in **exactly one** phase.

No duplicates across Validate / Build / Evolve / Support.

---

# Approved model

## Validate

1. **When**  
   They have an idea but need clarity before investing in design or development.

2. **Goal**  
   Create direction before build starts.

3. **Capabilities**  
   * Discovery Workshops  
   * Product Strategy  
   * User Research  
   * Technical Discovery  
   * AI Opportunity Scan  
   * Roadmapping  

4. **Examples**  
   * Validate an AI product concept  
   * Define the roadmap for a SaaS platform  
   * Technical due diligence before development  
   * Scope a complex platform  

5. **Outcome**  
   A validated product direction and clear execution plan.

---

## Build

1. **When**  
   They know what they want to build and need a senior product team to design and develop it.

2. **Goal**  
   Design and develop the product.

3. **Capabilities**  
   * UX/UI Design  
   * Product Engineering  
   * Front-end  
   * Back-end  
   * AI Integrations  
   * CMS Development  
   * Infrastructure  
   * QA & Launch  

4. **Examples**  
   * Headless website  
   * AI platform  
   * Customer portal  
   * Campaign platform  
   * Internal tooling  
   * SaaS product  

5. **Outcome**  
   A production-ready digital product.

---

## Evolve

1. **When**  
   Their product is live and they want to keep improving it.

2. **Goal**  
   Continuously improve a live product.

3. **Capabilities**  
   * Feature Development  
   * Product Optimisation  
   * AI Enhancements  
   * Performance  
   * Analytics  
   * Product Roadmapping  

4. **Examples**  
   * Add AI functionality  
   * Improve subscription flows  
   * Extend an existing CMS  
   * Optimise platform performance  

5. **Outcome**  
   A product that continuously improves.

---

## Support

1. **When**  
   They need a trusted technical partner to keep everything running.

2. **Goal**  
   Keep the product stable, secure and supported.

3. **Capabilities**  
   * Monitoring  
   * Maintenance  
   * Hosting  
   * Security  
   * Bug Fixes  
   * Technical Support  
   * Incident Response  

4. **Examples**  
   * Ongoing support after launch  
   * Hosting and infrastructure management  
   * Security updates  
   * Incident resolution  
   * Monthly maintenance  

5. **Outcome**  
   A stable, secure and supported digital product.

---

## Uniqueness check (flag only — do not resolve yet)

Possible overlaps to resolve when approving the model:

* Roadmapping (Validate) vs Product Roadmapping (Evolve)
* AI Integrations (Build) vs AI Enhancements (Evolve)

Agree the overall four-phase model first. Resolve overlaps only as part of approving this architecture — not as a content optimisation exercise.
