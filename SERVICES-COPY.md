## PART 3 — Case Study Template (Simple + "Nerd Out" Deep Dive)

Every case study now uses a two-layer structure. Visitors land on the
**simple version by default** — same fixed structure for every case, no
exceptions. A **"Want to nerd out?" button, fixed in position on screen**
(like a support widget — stays visible in the same spot while scrolling,
e.g. bottom-right) sits over the simple version. Clicking it reveals/loads
the existing, fully detailed case template (the one already built, e.g. the
SpilNews page with "The problem behind the brief," "How we built the right
thing," "Proof it worked," etc.) — that detailed template stays as-is and
does not need to be rebuilt or restructured. This is a persistent floating
button, not an inline link at the bottom of the page.

**Simple version — fixed structure for every case:**

1. Eyebrow: `CASE 0X / [Client] — [Year]`
2. Headline: the existing one-line case headline (unchanged from current case pages)
3. One large hero image of the product
4. Short summary: 1-2 sentences — what it is, who it's for
5. Metadata: Client / Year / Industry / Product URL / Service (e.g. "Build → Evolve")
6. Key Features: a short list of the product's core features (separate block, not folded into the summary)
7. 2-3 additional product images, shown without surrounding explanation
8. Client quote

*(Note: "Services" as a checklist of deliverable types — Logo, Brand Assets, Desktop Screens, Dashboard, CMS, Video, Wireframes, User Flows, Figma, Presentation, Photography, Animation — is an internal-only field for Karissa's own asset-selection use and is NOT part of the visitor-facing copy/template.)*
8. CTA button: **"Want to nerd out?"** → links to the full detailed case page

**Example (SpilNews):**

CASE 01 / SpilNews — 2025

**Video news platform for modern editorial teams.**

*[hero image]*

A dedicated video publishing platform for creating, managing, and distributing video-first news content.

Client: SpilNews
Year: 2025
Industry: [Media / Publishing]
Product URL: [link]
Service: Build → Evolve

Key Features:
- [Video content management]
- [Editorial scheduling & permissions]
- [Distribution workflow]

*[2-3 product images]*

"The platform gave our editorial team a clearer way to manage video-first publishing."
— Editorial lead, SpilNews

*(Throughout the simple version, a floating "Want to nerd out?" button stays fixed on screen, e.g. bottom-right — not just a CTA at the end of the page.)*
